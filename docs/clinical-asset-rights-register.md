## 1. Asset Strategy & Tiering Policy

To comply with international medical ethics, copyright law, and patient safeguarding:
- **Tier 1 (Public Default)**: **Custom Vector & Editorial Medical Illustrations**. High-contrast, dignified, non-graphic SVG artwork developed specifically for *Swasthya Katha*. Clear of third-party copyright restrictions.
- **Tier 2 (Educational & Staff Reference)**: **Verified Clinical Diagnostic Visuals**. Sourced from official public health educational publications (e.g. NACO Training Manuals, CDC Public Health Image Library [PHIL], WHO Syndromic Guidelines, and historical clinical slide references).
- **Sensitive Content Shield**: All Tier 2 clinical images are delivered behind a blur mask with clear, non-diagnostic content advisories.
