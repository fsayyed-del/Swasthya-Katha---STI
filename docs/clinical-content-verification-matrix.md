## 2. Claim-Level Educational Safety Verification
1. **Public Educational Claim (Tier 1 - Open)**:

2. **Public Clinical Scope (Tier 2 - Non-Diagnostic)**:

3. **Facilitator Protocol Claim (Tier 3 - Gated to Staff)**:
